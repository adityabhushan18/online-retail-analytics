About This Project

Built on the UCI Online Retail dataset (541,909 transactions, Dec 2010–Dec 2011, UK-based online gift retailer). Task 1 covers revenue trends, top products, regional performance and day/hour purchasing patterns. Task 2 segments customers using RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) quintile scoring into 8 named segments, cross-checked against an independent K-Means clustering model on log-transformed, standardized RFM values. All charts, KPIs and reports on this site are generated directly from the notebooks in notebooks/ — nothing here is hand-typed.
